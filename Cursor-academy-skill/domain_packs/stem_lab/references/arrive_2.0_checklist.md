# ARRIVE 2.0 Checklist: Essential 10 for Animal Research

## 1. Study design
- [ ] List the groups being compared, experimental unit, and sample size.

## 2. Sample size
- [ ] Specify the total number of animals used and how the sample size was decided.

## 3. Inclusion and exclusion criteria
- [ ] Define criteria for excluding animals/data and report any exclusions.

## 4. Randomisation
- [ ] Describe the method used to generate allocation sequences and allocate animals to groups.

## 5. Blinding
- [ ] Describe who was blinded to group allocation during experiment and assessment.

## 6. Outcome measures
- [ ] Clearly define primary and secondary outcome measures and methods used.

## 7. Statistical methods
- [ ] Provide details of statistical methods used for analysis.

## 8. Experimental animals
- [ ] Provide species, strain, sex, developmental stage, and source of animals.

## 9. Experimental procedures
- [ ] Detail what was done, when, how often, including drug doses and routes of administration.

## 10. Results
- [ ] Report outcome details for each group, including precision/variance indicators.
